REPAIR FAIR — PRINT AND FOLD
Print ONLY repair-fair.pdf, one page, on your one A4 cardstock sheet.
Choose A4, portrait, single-sided, Actual Size / 100%. Disable Fit, Shrink,
booklet, duplex and multiple pages per sheet. The page is 210 x 297 mm.

1. Lay the sheet printed side up. The upside-down words are intentional.
2. Measure 148.5 mm from either short edge on BOTH long edges. Make ONE
   straight cut across the full 210 mm width between those marks, along
   the solid grey centre line (extend it to the edges).
3. Each piece is 210 x 148.5 mm. Fold EACH piece once in half along its
   dashed line, 74.25 mm from either 210 mm edge. Bring the two 210 mm
   edges together with the PRINTING OUTSIDE. Crease firmly with the ruler.
   Do not cut the dashed lines.
4. Open each piece into an inverted V, fold at top, free 210 mm edges on
   the table. Spread the feet about 80 mm apart and adjust for stability.
5. Check from both sides: one tent reads CHECK-IN upright on each face;
   the other reads PICKUP upright on each face.

No tape, tabs or glue needed. Use indoors on a level dry table; this is
an unlocked V-fold and may slide if disturbed. Grey guides can remain.
The printed cut guide stops short of the sheet edges to allow printer
margins; the ruler measurements determine the full cut and folds.

EDITABLE SOURCE: repair-fair.svg, full-size vector layout. No edits needed.
Use the supplied PDF for printing. SVG font appearance can vary by editor
(Helvetica/Arial/sans-serif). build.py regenerates both files with Python 3
and PyMuPDF (pip install pymupdf); regeneration is NOT needed to print.
