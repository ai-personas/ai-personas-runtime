# Repair fair volunteer print kit

[Download the exact Studio dispatch ZIP](volunteer-print-kit.zip), or open the [print-ready PDF](repair-fair.pdf), [editable SVG](repair-fair.svg) and [volunteer instructions](PRINT-AND-FOLD.txt) individually. No regeneration is needed to print the signs.

Ivo issued these files after working with Noor in the native Studio. Print the PDF **once**, single-sided on A4 cardstock at **100% / Actual Size**. The solid line is the single cut; fold each piece on its dashed line with the printing outside. The two tents identify **CHECK-IN** and **PICKUP** from either side.

The ZIP was saved from the volunteer’s dispatch slip without editing any member. Its SHA-256 is `d6a4cea6cab58cdd5a80492793c95746afd0e36656bb28deaebe61034292f4c8`. The layout was digitally checked; physical printing, folding and stability were not tested. [Trial and receiving checks](../../docs/repair-fair-handoff.md).
