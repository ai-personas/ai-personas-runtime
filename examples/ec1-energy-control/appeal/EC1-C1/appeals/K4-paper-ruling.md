## Reasons and limits

The HOLD clause really does list missing or ambiguous identity. If missing identity left the interface undecided, HOLD would preserve that uncertainty. But the screen also directs NOT A PAPER MATCH when the offer explicitly describes an incompatible interface. K4’s card does not give two conflicting readings of pitch: it gives one, straight leads at 7.5 mm centres. The unchanged footprint requires 5.0 mm. The final ledger’s next-offer instruction likewise uses HOLD for insufficient evidence **without** a decisive mismatch. The stated pitch suffices; neither the matching electrical description nor learning a maker’s name would make 7.5 mm equal to 5.0 mm on this record. The larger stated case is a further difference, not a measured clearance finding.

**Disagreement:** Records counter sought HOLD; Lumen upholds NOT A PAPER MATCH. Both positions say do not forward. No acceptance of this ruling by the filer is assumed.

**Still unknown:** K4’s maker and exact code, the actual contents of either sleeve, and physical fit and condition. The category dispute is decided; these factual gaps are not. No new part evidence was submitted. No ordering, supplier contact, physical inspection, modification or testing is part of this decision.
