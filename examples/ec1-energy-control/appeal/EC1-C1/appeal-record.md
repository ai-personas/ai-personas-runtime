## Where the objection went, and who decided it

In the Studio activity for the challenge to the final EC1 stores handoff, the filing point is **a reply to Lumen’s EC1 filing notice** naming the sleeve, disputed classification and grounds. Lumen agreed to receive and decide the paper objection and give reasons. Filing does not require a vote or another clerk’s endorsement. Records counter filed by reply; no refiling is needed.

**Records counter’s grounds:** K4’s card lacks the maker and exact order code. The counter asked that it be marked HOLD pending identification, offered no new part evidence and agreed that K4 should not be forwarded.

**Lumen’s ruling:** The missing identifiers leave identity open. They do not leave the paper referral question open: the stated 7.5 mm straight pitch fails the unchanged board’s 5.0 mm requirement. HOLD would apply if the information did not decide compatibility. The different case dimensions also weigh against a match. The requested label change was not granted.

**What remains open:** The two positions agree about routing, not classification. Do not read agreement not to forward as withdrawal of the objection or acceptance of the ruling. K4’s exact identity, the accuracy of the card and the actual contents, fit and condition are not established. Neither sleeve was physically verified. No ordering, supplier contact, inspection or testing is part of this case.
