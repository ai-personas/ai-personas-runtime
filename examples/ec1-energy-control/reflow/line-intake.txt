REFLOW DESK — DIGITAL INTAKE
This is the contract for this receiving desk, not a claim about a particular factory's machine zero or its acceptance of a job.

We kit by exact manufacturer, manufacturer part number and C-number in catalog.sqlite (frozen public JLC/LCSC catalog mirror, https://github.com/yaqwsx/jlcparts/tree/2abff37b711d8a24f686d949cc5219067b26c48c/data ; SHA-256 080b01786a73850ce546a049053dbcf2c15fc41b029bd69b6c942d1848b56632). Catalog quantities have their own observation dates: neither a reservation nor a live stock check. No need to put this whole database in the delivery. No parts are preselected on our side.

BOM.csv: one row per fitted designator, UTF-8, comma-separated, header Ref,Manufacturer,MPN,Catalog. Each board gets one of each row. Exclude etched features and external equipment. State the total lot requirement separately. Positions.csv: one row per fitted designator, header Ref,X_mm,Y_mm,Rotation_deg,Side. Side=Top. All X and Y coordinates are millimetres from the finished board lower-left viewed from the component side; +X right, +Y up. Rotation is positive counterclockwise on that view. Use component body center. For our feeder library, 0 degrees places a polarized two-terminal part's POSITIVE end to the LEFT, or a nonpolar two-terminal part's long axis horizontally (ends interchangeable). Document the physical polarity marking and show how the placement lines map to the board pins. If another kind of package is used, supply the corresponding zero drawing. These are our line coordinates, not necessarily a CAD footprint angle, packaging-tape orientation or supplier default. Use the same physical datum for the probe map; document the CAM origin if it differs. A genuine blocker should stay visible; don't invent a supplier or shop signoff.
