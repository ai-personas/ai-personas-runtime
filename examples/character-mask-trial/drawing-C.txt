One flat opaque card mask, 0.8 mm black card, laser cut. All dimensions mm.

control acceptance. Deliver a 1:1 editable cut SVG and shop directions.

FRONT VIEW / DATUM: top left of each finished rectangle; +X right, +Y down.
The mask and an underlying printed reader card key against the same two
stops. The installed mask datum relative to the card datum can translate
by +/-0.20 in X and +/-0.15 in Y, independently. The stops prevent rotation.
Use the specified front view, without mirroring.

MASK blank: finished 96.00 +/-0.20 wide by 54.00 +/-0.20 high. Square
corners. Two rectangular through windows parallel to the blank axes.
Only the perimeter and the two windows are cut. Keep at least 3.00 mm of
actual opaque material between windows and between a window and the outside.

FUNCTION: Each window must expose its entire printed rectangle with at
least 0.15 mm clear on each of the four sides. Each opening must stay at
least 0.15 mm inside all four edges of its own clear zone. These margins
apply at every permitted combination, including registration and cutting.

SHOP PROCESS: On the finished mask, each of the four edges of each opening
can differ from its artwork coordinate relative to the finished mask datum
by +/-0.10 mm. Each finished overall mask dimension can differ from its
artwork dimension by +/-0.12 mm. These allowances already include kerf
handling. All toleranced variables may vary independently. Do not add a
second kerf offset to the artwork. Use ordinary shop settings for the stock.
