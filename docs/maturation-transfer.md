### Repair

On an eligible reminder or revisit cue, `after_your_observation` supplies the **owner’s forward prediction** from the current question edition, how the owner classified that earlier finding, and the exact question, application and outcome it can choose to read together. It binds the prediction to the accepted `development.update`, its issuing receipt, the actual applied choice and the observation the interpreting decision saw. It does not replay the old brief, the reader’s words or the owner’s private explanation. A contrary finding is as eligible as a consistent one. A closed question, a pending but unjudged reply, or an out-of-scope revisit does not become a lesson. A bad optional binding withholds the prediction, not the current task.

**Supply is not use.** Studio’s question history now shows **Own prediction supplied** separately from an explicit application. Seeing the addresses does not supply the underlying records for an appraisal. The owner can use or ignore the prediction without writing a new plan or changing its character. [Interface](editable-persona-models.md#numerical-workspaces).

### What the assessment verifier checks

For this study we checked the returned bytes independently of the parsed assessment. On disconnected copies, deleting or damaging a declared provider-output archive still let the old `neural verify-assessment` report success: it checked the shared inputs, numerical evaluation and local response against completion records, but not those separately retained final bytes. The original outputs were intact; this did not change either study’s result.

The verifier now checks the authorizing decision for operation-bound assessments and each comparison arm against its retained provider output. A missing or corrupt declared archive, or a different identifiable final answer, fails verification. Where the final bytes cannot be independently matched, the report distinguishes that from a match. On nine existing assessments, **37/37** arm responses matched the provider finals; all six assessments in the operation-bound format also matched the authorizing choice. The five damaged-archive copies that previously passed now failed. An older-format fixture with no archive declared still verified its recorded comparison but did not count that arm as a provider-final match. This checks the evidence used to judge a revision; it does not judge whether the revision helped.

### When one earlier decision cannot be verified

On Rhea’s original-character history just after Alder, a work-filtered `persona.revision_uses` query verified her handoff. The **same candidate** on the default six-decision page returned only an error. One older projection could not be verified exactly, so the tool discarded the later handoff too. Some early compact inputs lack the current contract field and even differ from their full forward trace in the last digit. Rounding them into matches would misstate what was supplied.

Revision inspection now keeps verified decisions and lists unavailable index entries separately, with a reason and **no numerical or outcome claim**. The default page returned the same one verified decision and five unavailable entries. All 35 index positions were accounted for across pages. On a disconnected copy of the revised-character history, removing a current decision’s declared provider final left three verified decisions and one unavailable, not four changed or one equal. An invalid candidate or switch still fails the whole query; a follow-up about an individual use still needs that exact verified choice. This fixes access to evidence across time. Neither persona used the inspector in the packet jobs, and the fix does not change those results.
