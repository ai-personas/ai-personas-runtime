# What the runtime demonstrates against the 46 user requirements

**November 25, 2026.** The [published index][design] has 46 active requirements. This is an audit of those outcomes, not a count of implemented features. The [44 mechanical, behavioral and conditional acceptance criteria](acceptance-map.md) are a separate catalogue. Neither table is a product-wide pass certificate.

**Verdict:** continuing personas can take a brief in Studio, choose collaborators and tools, deliver files that a separate receiver can use unchanged, and stop without requiring a human to take an outside step. A human can also own or contribute to the task. There is direct evidence of those behaviors. **Useful, persistent development caused by a freely adopted numerical change is still not demonstrated.** In particular, a successful second project, a remembered practice and a different model output are not interchangeable evidence.

## Where verification stops

The [original workshop and fabrication ZIPs][ec1] need no CAD edit for the stock KiCad and CAM checks performed. They are for bare-board fabrication and hand assembly, **not** an assembled order. No supplier upload was submitted, no order was placed, no board was built, and no load or force was measured. The independent [owner/contributor usability protocol](../examples/owner-contributor/) still needs actual independent participants; two windows under evaluator control cannot supply their experiences.

For the numerical question, the missing evidence is a persona’s voluntary, experience-linked **adopted** change, a later ordinary choice that actually uses changed values, and a better consequence relative to its earlier self under comparable conditions. A matched task must also check for lost useful behavior. More calls on a stopped activity, an operator-authored coefficient or a favorable profile screenshot cannot fill this gap.
