# What the runtime demonstrates against the 46 user requirements

**November 27, 2026.** The [published index][design] has 46 active requirements. This is an audit of those outcomes, not a count of implemented features. The [44 mechanical, behavioral and conditional acceptance criteria](acceptance-map.md) are a separate catalogue. Neither table is a product-wide pass certificate.

**Verdict:** continuing personas can take a brief in Studio, choose collaborators and tools, deliver files that a separate receiver can use unchanged, and stop without requiring a human to take an outside step. A human can also own or contribute to the task. There is direct evidence of those behaviors. **Useful, persistent development caused by a freely adopted numerical change is still not demonstrated.** In particular, a successful second project, a remembered practice and a different model output are not interchangeable evidence.

There were also 17 rejected responses and one attempt without a response. These counts exclude the first 92 completed calls, which are already included in the multi-activity evidence below. The [fixed PCB, simulations and energy accounting](../examples/exodus-design-room/fixed/) are available for digital inspection; the [notice to the recipient](../examples/exodus-design-room/fixed/DO-NOT-ORDER.txt) says not to order the hardware.
