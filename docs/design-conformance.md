# November design implementation and live observations

The numerical Studio runs continuing personas through direct provider calls. Real participants produced editable designs, exchanged messages, reused other projects’ files and completed a reversible character experiment. **Beneficial self-directed numerical maturation over ordinary work remains unproven.** In the five-person cohort, all five authored and used initial models. Three retained developmental questions after feedback. None proposed a subsequent numerical revision across the eleven activities below.

## What a character change actually did

Ren's separate supervised study started when an ambiguous reading-group message elicited an assumption about travel. The evaluator requested a comparison between clarifying ambiguous assent and responding to clear assent. Ren authored a literal edit and selected matched probes. In the first comparison the clarification output moved from 0.32748 to 0.67507 under ambiguity. The clear case had no forward difference. Ren judged the two arms substantively similar and retained the original character.

In an explicitly requested reversible crossover, Ren assessed a second candidate and activated it. A new social activity actually used the temporary package in epoch 2. Its reply treated Saturday as settled after tentative assent. The evaluator, acting as host, recorded that specific problem and acknowledged the offered alternative. Ren restored the byte-identical original model in epoch 3 and linked the earlier adoption, later decision and received feedback. Its recorded disposition was **uncertain**. The temporary model had received baseline inputs for that reply, leaving its conditional edit untested in that situation.

Under the restored model, another supervised exchange exercised the contextual path. Ren used the speaker’s pending family confirmation as evidence for uncertainty of 0.65. The following request supplied the twelve evaluated outputs and Ren kept Saturday open. An explicit resumption reused that exact evaluation. When the host amended the brief to confirm availability, the next input withheld the stale outputs and named the changed dependency. Ren appraised the new evidence, then acknowledged the confirmed time in a later reply. This sequence establishes evaluation, actual use, reuse and invalidation. The earlier tentative reply and later improved reply used different incoming facts and different model states.

A third assessment replayed two earlier encounters. On Ren’s tentative Saturday invitation, the original appraisal changed the candidate’s clarification output by +0.190534 relative to the active package. The active, candidate and control without numerical inclinations all left Saturday provisional. In the loaner-desk encounter, the two arms had equal numerical outputs. Both read 92%, 79% and 68% at the correct times from the same verified PNG and declined to promise evening coverage. The original responses and subsequent outcomes were withheld from the replay inputs. The new Studio comparison view exposes the shared evidence and each actual response.

The study produced three completed assessments with thirteen matched-arm provider calls in total. It establishes that a persona can author, compare, decline, adopt, use and roll back an actual numerical revision. It leaves the benefit of that revision, spontaneous ordinary maturation and preservation of individuality through beneficial change unresolved.
